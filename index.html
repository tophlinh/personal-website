About

I build recommendation systems at Disney — the algorithms that decide what millions of people see when they open Disney+ and Hulu. I think about user behavior, content diversity, and the quiet power of defaults.

Outside of that, I draw things and sell them at conventions. I co-founded a bouldering gym. I’m pursuing graduate research in human-centered design because I’m interested in what these systems do to people, not just whether they work.

This site is a small place for both halves to exist together.
